CashFlow privacy notice

CashFlow is a personal finance application. Its ledger and imported transactions are stored in the application’s local database on the device. A backup can leave the device when its owner chooses to export or share it.

The optional Wise integration uses Enable Banking to request account identifiers, currencies and transaction history for accounts the device owner authorizes. Enable Banking and Wise handle bank sign-in and consent on their own pages under their own privacy notices. CashFlow stores the owner’s API configuration in Android secure storage and contacts Enable Banking directly for subsequent updates. A CashFlow server is not involved.

The owner can stop future updates by removing the connection in CashFlow, and revoke bank access with Wise or Enable Banking. Previously imported operations can be managed in the local ledger.
