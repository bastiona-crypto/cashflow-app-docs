CashFlow terms for personal use

CashFlow records and displays personal financial information. The optional Wise integration requests account information for personal accounts the device owner is authorized to access. It cannot initiate payments or move funds.

Connecting Wise is optional. Use of the integration is subject to the terms and authorization settings of Wise and Enable Banking. The owner can withdraw consent at any time. Updates depend on active consent, network access and provider availability.

CashFlow is a recordkeeping tool. The account provider’s official statement remains the source for reconciling transactions and balances.
